About Us

Where Passion Meets Performance

Padeltaz is more than a club — it's a community built around the love of padel. From beginners to pros, everyone finds their place here.

The Ultimate Padel Destination

Founded in 2019, Padeltaz has grown into one of the region's premier padel destinations. Our state-of-the-art facilities feature 12 professional-grade courts with premium lighting for night play.

Whether you're looking for casual games, competitive leagues, or professional coaching, we provide everything you need to take your game to the next level.
