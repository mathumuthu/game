Our Journey

The Timeline

From a single court to a premier sports academy — our journey of growth.

2019

The Beginning

Padeltaz opened its doors with 2 outdoor courts and a small team of 3 coaches. The vision was simple: make padel accessible to everyone.

2020

First Expansion

Added 4 indoor courts with climate control and premium lighting. Membership grew to 500 active players.

2021

Academy Launch

Launched the Padeltaz Academy with structured coaching programs for juniors and adults. Hosted our first regional tournament.

2022

Pro Shop & Cafe

Opened our pro shop with premium equipment and a cafe lounge. Membership surpassed 1,500 players.

2023

Fitness Center

Added a dedicated fitness center with padel-specific training equipment. Expanded to 12 total courts.

2024

2,500+ Members

Reached 2,500 active members and hosted 48 tournaments. Became the region's leading padel destination.
