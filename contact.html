FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our club, membership, and services.

How do I book a court?

You can book a court through our online booking system, mobile app, or by calling our reception at +1 (555) 123-4567. Members can book up to 14 days in advance, while non-members can book up to 7 days ahead.

What are your membership options?

We offer three membership tiers: Basic ($49/month), Pro ($99/month), and Elite ($179/month). Each tier includes different benefits such as court bookings, coaching sessions, and gym access. Visit our pricing page for full details.

Do you provide equipment?

Yes! We provide basic rackets and balls for beginners during their first sessions. We also have a pro shop with premium equipment from top brands, and members receive a 15% discount on all purchases.

Can I try before I join?

Absolutely! We offer a free trial session for first-time visitors. You can book a complimentary 30-minute court session and meet with one of our coaches to discuss your goals. Contact us to schedule your trial.

Do you offer coaching for kids?

Yes, our Junior Academy accepts children aged 6-16. We have age-appropriate programs that focus on skill development, fitness, and fun. Our junior coaches are specially trained to work with young players.

What is your cancellation policy?

Court bookings can be cancelled up to 24 hours before your scheduled time for a full refund. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a cancellation fee. Memberships can be paused or cancelled with 30 days notice.
